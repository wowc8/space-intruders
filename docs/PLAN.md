Chosen rules: 11 columns, 5 rows; top row 30, next two rows 20, bottom two 10. Three lives, one extra life once at score >=1500. One active player projectile; held fire permitted as modern adaptation. Four cell-based bunkers eroded by shots from either side and contact with aliens. Enemy shots from living lowest alien in a chosen column, bounded concurrent count and seeded randomness. Saucer crosses periodically, score seeded from 50/100/150/300; simplified timer/random selection is intentional adaptation. Formation accelerates with eliminated aliens and each cleared wave; descend at edge based on living bounds. Invasion ends run. After damage clear nearby bullets and give short respawn invulnerability; no multiple losses in one tick. Clear wave gives brief transition then fresh bunkers/formation, carries score/lives. Restart produces clean initial state. Pause freezes simulation. Blur/visibility loss auto pauses and releases held keys.
